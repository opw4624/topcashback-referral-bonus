## How the Tell-a-Friend Bonus Actually Works

The flow is straightforward, but each step has a rule attached that can quietly disqualify a referral.

1. **You share your unique Tell-a-Friend link.** The link is tied to your account so TopCashback knows who to credit.
2. **Your friend signs up through that link.** If they navigate to TopCashback on their own and sign up, no referral is recorded. The link has to be the entry point.
3. **Your friend earns $10 in Cash Back.** This is the threshold. Not $10 spent — $10 in tracked Cash Back, which depending on the rate can mean anywhere from a $50 purchase at 20% back to a $1,000 purchase at 1% back.
4. **The Cash Back moves from "Pending" to "Confirmed" to "Payable."** This is where most of the waiting happens. Pending means the transaction was tracked. Confirmed means the retailer has reviewed the purchase and confirmed the criteria were met. Payable means the retailer's affiliate network has invoiced and the funds have been received by TopCashback. Only at the Payable stage does your referral bonus unlock.
5. **Both of you must have verified your email addresses.** If either side skipped the email authentication step, the bonus stays invisible until that's fixed.
6. **Your bonus appears, labeled "TopCashback Referral," with the date your friend signed up.** You can withdraw it via PayPal, ACH/Direct Deposit, Venmo, or redeem it for a gift card (which comes with up to a 5% bonus).

The 180-day clock starts when your friend signs up, not when they make their first purchase. That's worth keeping in mind — a friend who signs up in January and only does their first TopCashback shopping trip in July is cutting it close.

## Common Reasons Your Tell-a-Friend Bonus Hasn't Appeared

Reddit threads and the TopCashback help pages fill up with questions from confused members who referred a friend, saw them sign up, watched them shop — and then never saw a bonus. Almost every case traces back to one of these issues.

**1. Your friend's Cash Back hasn't reached "Payable" status yet.** This is the big one. Your bonus only unlocks once your friend's $10 in Cash Back moves from Confirmed to Payable. That final step depends on the retailer's affiliate network sending an invoice to TopCashback, which can take anywhere from a few weeks to several months. If your friend's Cash Back is still sitting at Confirmed, you'll see them on your Referral Performance page, but no bonus yet.

**2. Either of you hasn't verified your email.** TopCashback requires both the referrer and the referred member to authenticate their email addresses. If your friend signed up but never clicked the verification link in the welcome email, the referral sits in limbo until they do.

**3. Your friend didn't use your link to sign up.** If they typed "topcashback.com" into their browser and signed up that way, no referral is recorded. The link has to be the actual entry point.

**4. The $10 came from an excluded source.** Sign-up bonuses, New Member Deals, goodwill credits, contest winnings, and cash back from the excluded-merchants list (covered below) do **not** count toward the $10 threshold. Your friend might think they've hit it because their account balance shows $10+, but if any of that came from excluded sources, the threshold hasn't actually been met.

**5. The 180-day window expired.** As of January 6, 2025, Tell-a-Friend bonuses carry an expiration date — typically 4 to 12 months depending on the bonus type, with 180 days being the standard for the Tell-a-Friend program. If your friend hasn't earned the required Cash Back by then, the bonus expires and gets reallocated to other offers on the site. You can't appeal this; you just have to invite someone else.

**6. The referral was deemed not genuine.** If TopCashback's fraud-prevention system flags the signup as suspicious — multiple accounts from the same person, suspicious IP patterns, signups from blocked regions — both accounts can be terminated and the bonus voided.

You can check the status of every referral on your **Referral Performance page** inside your account dashboard. It will show your friend's username, the date they joined, and where they are in the process.

## Tell a Friend FAQ

**Can I refer family members?**

Yes. The terms explicitly acknowledge that husband-and-wife and other family arrangements exist. The one rule: one account per person. Setting up multiple accounts under your own name to refer yourself is fraud and will get all linked accounts terminated.

**Is there a limit on how many people I can refer?**

No. The official May 2026 promo language says "There's no limit to how many friends you can invite." The rewards scale linearly — refer ten qualifying friends at $40 each and you've earned $400.

**What if my friend already has a TopCashback account?**

You can't refer existing members. Tell-a-Friend only pays for brand-new signups that come through your link.

**Can I use my Tell-a-Friend link in Google AdWords or other paid ads?**

No. The terms specifically forbid using referral links in search engine sponsored listings or paid advertising without prior written permission from TopCashback. Violating this can result in account termination and forfeiture of any earned Cash Back.

**How long until my referral bonus becomes payable?**

It depends entirely on how long your friend's qualifying Cash Back takes to move from Confirmed to Payable. Some retailers pay within weeks; others take months. The bonus lands in your account once your friend's $10 hits Payable status and both emails are verified.

**Do I earn Cash Back on my friend's purchases forever?**

No. Tell-a-Friend is a one-time bonus per qualifying referral, not an ongoing commission. Once your friend hits the $10 threshold and your bonus unlocks, that's it for that referral — invite them again and you get nothing further.

**What payment methods can I use to withdraw my referral bonus?**

PayPal, ACH/Direct Deposit (US bank accounts only), Venmo, and 40+ gift card options including Amazon and American Express Reward Card. Gift card redemptions come with up to a 5% bonus on top. There's no minimum withdrawal amount.

**If I referred a friend before January 6, 2025, does the expiration date apply?**

No. TopCashback has confirmed that referrals made before the expiration-date policy was added are still honored, so long as the friend has earned the required Cash Back. If you have an old referral that's stuck, submit a support ticket and they'll assist.
